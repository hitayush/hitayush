I’m a full-stack developer who enjoys taking an idea from its first sketch to a polished, usable product. I’m especially drawn to projects where thoughtful engineering can make a real difference—whether that means shaping a smooth user experience, working through the logic behind the scenes, or finding the story hidden inside data.

Right now, I’m growing at the intersection of **full-stack development**, **AI/ML**, and **data analysis**. I like asking practical questions, learning quickly, and building answers that are clear, useful, and human-centred.

## What I’m building

### 🩺 [DialDoctor.in](https://dialdoctor.in)

**DialDoctor.in** is a project close to the kind of work I want to do more of: using technology to make essential services feel more accessible and approachable. It reflects my interest in solving everyday problems with product-minded development, not just writing code in isolation.

### 🎓 Student Attendance Tracker

I’m also building a **Student Attendance Tracker**—a practical tool intended to make attendance management simpler, more organised, and easier to understand. It is an opportunity to combine full-stack problem-solving with a real, everyday workflow.
